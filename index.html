Briefing layer for on-call alerts

AlertBrief

The alert already reaches the right person. This decides what they read when it does: is it real, what probably happened, what to check first, and what else is firing. Every fact comes from the alert itself or the team's own alert history. Nothing is configured per customer and nothing is invented.

All data synthetic. No real system is represented.
